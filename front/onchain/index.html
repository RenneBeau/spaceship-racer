Reward estimates

SRT: your earned cycle points divided by total earned points, multiplied by the current SRT pool.

SpaceUSD: clans receive a share based on their points. After the leader premium, members split that share by ship levels used across their participating races. Solo players use their own point share. Maintenance is deducted; leader and referral income are added.

These are estimates if the cycle settled at current totals. SpaceUSD keeps accruing from the reserve; future wins, upgrades and boosts change the result. Claimable rewards are separate.

Races

Your fleet enters automatically. More distance gives you more weight in the winner draw. The winner receives cycle points; rewards settle after the cycle ends.

Ship upgrades and equipment changes apply next race.

Boosts

Overdrive adds speed. Afterburner builds speed over time. Warp adds temporary distance. Rockets damage a randomly selected opponent.

Repair restores HP and shields and can revive fallen ships. More damage costs more; higher tank levels lower the cost. Fallen ships add a surcharge. You choose a spending limit and can recover unused SRT.

Each purchase belongs to a race window. If the race ended earlier, recover the unused SRT. Network gas is separate.
