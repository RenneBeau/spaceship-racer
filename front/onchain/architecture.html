SPACESHIP RACER

How the game works

You own a permanent fleet. Races use a snapshot of that fleet. The contracts hold the assets and enforce the rules; the website makes them easier to use.

RACES

Race flow

  1. 1

    Build your fleet

    Buy ships, level them up and equip commanders or skins.

    Permanent ownership
  2. 2

    Start a race

    The game takes your active levels and equipment as its starting snapshot.

    Fair starting state
  3. 3

    Choose your boosts

    Spend SRT on speed, acceleration, warp or rockets. Ending opportunities occur every six seconds.

    Player decisions
  4. 4

    Resolve & repeat

    The game replays actions, resolves combat and picks the winner. Cycle rewards accumulate for claims.

    Contract-enforced result
Why is the optimized version cheaper?

Most of the race records actions and checks whether it ends. It does not rewrite every fleet's movement on every tick. Movement and combat are replayed in bounded batches at the end. The indexer calculates previews for the website; only the on-chain replay determines the outcome. There is no regeneration.

SHIPS

Upgrading your fleet

Upgrades are permanent, but they do not change a race already in progress.

  1. 1

    Choose a target level

    Example: your fighter is level 18 and you choose level 19.

  2. 2

    Pay the difference

    The current price is 1 SpaceUSD per added level. You sign the purchase in your wallet.

  3. 3

    Upgrade is queued

    You own level 19, but the current race still uses its level-18 snapshot.

  4. 4

    Next race uses level 19

    The fleet becomes active at the race boundary and the race engine refreshes its ship stats.

Checked on the live local chain

On November 3, 2026, the browser test confirmed a fighter upgrade from 18 → 19: race 286 kept level 18, while level 19 was queued for race 287. This is a verification snapshot, not a live race counter.

What about multiple upgrades or invalid purchases?

Buying 18 → 19 and then 19 → 20 before the next race charges only each additional level. The next race uses level 20. Levels must increase and stay within 1–1000. Buying an already owned ship, upgrading an unowned ship and incorrect payments are rejected. First-purchase referral discounts are separate from standard upgrade pricing.

ARCHITECTURE

Contracts and services

Each group handles a different part of the game. Expand a card for details.

01

Race engine

Runs races, verifies randomness, resolves actions and tracks cycle points.

Contracts

Race engine (code 48), stateless tick verifier (code 47) and the randomness adapter. They verify future drand proofs; the keeper cannot choose the random result.

02

Ships and equipment

Stores ship levels and equipped items, including changes queued for next race.

Contracts

Fleet stores permanent levels; equipment holds equipped NFTs and processes changes at boundaries. The race engine reads these to build race snapshots.

03

Commanders, skins and trading

Handles draws, fusion, skin rewards and player listings.

Contracts

Recruitment/fusion, commander collection, milestone skins, skin collection and marketplace. Equipment custody and marketplace sale escrow are separate.

04

Payments and rewards

Processes purchases, holds reward reserves and records claimable balances.

Contracts

Payments routes supported tokens through the CL8Y DEX to settle SpaceUSD invoices. Rewards holds funded reserves and claims. Capital treasury converts revenue and dispatches capital to Collapsar, separately from prize reserves. Payment tokens: cLUNC, cUSTC, UST1, SpaceUSD and SRT.

05

Teams & controls

Handles clan membership and the shared emergency pause.

Contracts

Clans tracks current and queued memberships and integrates with cycle scoring. Pause is checked by configured game modules and NFT collections.

06

Website, indexer and keeper

Displays game data, indexes history and submits pending race transactions.

Off-chain services

Your wallet signs purchases and actions. The read-only indexer/API uses SQLite and calculates previews. The keeper relays proofs and executes bounded work with its own LUNC gas balance. A health timer checks chain freshness and index lag.

ECONOMICS

Boost fees and gas costs

You pay SRT for boosts

The race contract holds it until it knows which actions were used.

95%

Prize allocation

Of executed boost spending.

5%

Operator fee

Of executed boost spending, paid in SRT.

Unused

Refundable to you

Unused boost payments earn no operator fee.

The keeper pays LUNC gas.

SRT fee revenue does not automatically become LUNC. The economic target is for the realizable value of the 5% fee to cover all keeper gas. Equal hourly activity must be measured to prove that.
